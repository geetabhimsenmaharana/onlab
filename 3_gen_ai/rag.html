Generation.

A way to give an AI system relevant information before it generates an answer.

01

THE IDEA

What is RAG?

A language model can generate an answer from what it learned during training. RAG adds another step: before answering, the system retrieves relevant information from an external knowledge source and gives that information to the model.

The goal is to help the model generate a response using information that is relevant to the question and available at the time of the request.

MY NOTES

What I understand so far.

RAG connects retrieval with generation.

The quality of the retrieved information can affect the quality of the final answer.

RAG is useful when an AI system needs access to information that may not be contained in the model's original training data.
