Large Language
Models.

AI models trained on large amounts of data that can process and generate language.

01

THE IDEA

What is an LLM?

LLM stands for Large Language Model.

An LLM is a machine learning model designed to work with language. It can process text and generate text based on patterns learned during training.

Examples of tasks include answering questions, summarizing text, translating languages, writing code, and generating other forms of text.

02

A SIMPLE ANALOGY

Think of autocomplete.

Imagine typing a sentence on your phone.

"The sun rises in the..."

The system may predict that the next word could be "east".

An LLM does something much more sophisticated. It uses patterns learned from large amounts of training data to predict and generate sequences of tokens.
